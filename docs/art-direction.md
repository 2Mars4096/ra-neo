# Art direction: painted military miniatures

Selected by the user on 2026-10-13. Recognizable modern equipment and carefully painted tabletop terrain are the reference idea. Keep the camera readable and the mood playful without losing military silhouettes.

## Current implementation
- Olive friendly equipment and ochre enemy equipment; charcoal running gear, blue-black glass, restrained identification marks.
- Shared matte paint across equipment and solid scenery, broad surface variation, lighter upper faces, subtle lower-surface dust, softened light bands.
- Warm directional light and cool ambient fill.
- Coast: stone ridge, warehouses, harbor. Fields: ochre crop parcels, barns, shelter belts, exposed routes. Urban: cool asphalt, flat-roof blocks, cross streets, rubble.
- Preserve independent turrets, limbs, wheels and rotors. Tanks have no synthetic flat-ground chassis vibration.

## Review criteria
A unit class and faction should remain identifiable at normal zoom; the scene should have quiet ground, readable obstacles, and focal units. Surface detail must not sparkle or overwhelm silhouettes. New geometry must remain within tested collision envelopes.

## Remaining production work
This is an initial material/lighting pass over procedural models. Custom painted textures, stronger architecture variety, authored foliage, refined infantry anatomy/poses, richer props, and a matching effects/audio treatment remain future work. The maps still share their outer footprint and eastern water boundary.
