# 1-2: Authentic equipment and environment

**Parent:** [1: Tactical RTS vertical slice](1-tactical-rts-vertical-slice.md)
**Status:** completed
**Goal:** Replace toy-like blockouts with readable, military-inspired equipment while preserving clearance and runtime performance.

- [x] Rebuild tanks/AA with angled hulls and turrets, road wheels, track links, optics, hatches, and guns.

- [x] Finish final material/render inspection and benchmark; record evidence and launch the MVP.

## Decisions

Authenticity means recognizable proportions, functional equipment, matte materials, and clear silhouettes at RTS zoom. It does not mean exact real-world dimensions or a licensed replica. Original procedural geometry remains the asset pipeline for this MVP.

The close-up equipment study and battle screenshots are complementary review artifacts: detail that disappears at play zoom cannot carry unit identification by itself.
