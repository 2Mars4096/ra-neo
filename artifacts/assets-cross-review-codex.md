## Five prioritized fixes

1. **P1 — Make placement context enforceable.** `scripts/asset_library.gd:63–77` checks map enclosure, water versus land, selected support flags and reserved rectangles, but never reads `placement` or `entrance`. With an in-bounds position and empty reservations, a headwall on arbitrary land or a shelter with its opening facing away from access gets no context objection (static source inference, not executed). Street metadata already identifies bank/ditch contexts, shelter entrances and discharge direction. Add explicit receiving context and transformed access/discharge zones; reject incompatible context and blocked access. Preserve the distinction between authoring validation and installed gameplay collision. Acceptance: headwalls require a receiving drainage edge, and shelters retain a clear entrance-to-footway connection.

2. **P1 — Describe ground supports separately from overhead spans.** Both overhead-cable variants include their own ground supports (`assets_street.gd:727–777`); both gantries have ground columns/chassis (`assets_waterfront.gd:59–78`). Their metadata nevertheless has only `collision: overhead`, and placement reserves the entire 2D envelope without height/part information. The gallery confirms substantial supports. This is an ambiguous future placement contract, not evidence of a current walk-through bug: library builds explicitly install no collision. Add ground-blocker footprints and an overhead clearance envelope, or explicitly mark the complete assembly non-traversable pending integration. Reserve `requires_external_support` for variants that actually need it; the current kind-wide support check also includes these self-supported cable assemblies. Acceptance: an author can distinguish a blocked pole/base from the space beneath the wire/beam without interpreting prose.

3. **P2 — Fix dimension semantics and provide a useful inspection view.** Industry labels call pallets and drums 2.5 units tall; town-life labels do the same for bench/bin. `assets_existing.gd:15` supplies this common conservative height, while `roadside_props.gd:133–158` builds pallets to 0.64, drums to 1.0, bench to 1.005 and bin to 0.895. The gallery presents these as dimensions and uses them to frame the camera (`tools/asset_gallery.gd:66–71`), leaving small props undersized. Separate measured render bounds from conservative placement envelopes and label each accurately. Add a selectable enlarged card with front/rear rotation and ID/description/placement details. The current fixed +Z view hides the cabinet's -Z door/vents, and long spans make cables and hoist details difficult to judge. Acceptance: accurate displayed dimensions, inspectable service faces, and a separate fixed-scale comparison for gameplay readability.

4. **P2 — Give the glazed shelter an architectural glass treatment.** In town_life, its large back/end panes read as opaque blue panels. `assets_street.gd:506–516` uses vehicle glass; `unit_models.gd:13,25` and `shaders/miniature.gdshader` provide dark color/specular response but no transparency. This explains the observed appearance; the glazing geometry is present. Use a restrained shelter-specific glass treatment that keeps the bench/interior and background legible through the large panes. Keep vehicle glazing independent. Acceptance remains a rendered judgment from front and side at intended scale; this review does not prescribe an unmeasured expensive transparency solution.

5. **P3 — Break the headwall's ornamental rock border.** The utilities capture shows rip-rap as a near-even necklace around the apron. `assets_street.gd:803–809` places eight stones at 0.38 spacing with only ±0.05 depth jitter, then mirrored three-stone side runs. Individual rocks vary, but the arrangement exposes the generator and looks landscaped rather than deposited for scour protection. Compose an irregular, locally dense apron-edge patch with varied grouping, orientation and exposed ground; keep the outlet/apron clear and the existing bounds. Acceptance: recognizable erosion protection without a bead-like perimeter. Preserve concrete, flap valve and handrail detail.
