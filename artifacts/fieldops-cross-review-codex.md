# Field operations UI / visual cross-review

Read-only review, 2026-10-14. Only this report was written. No tests, Godot/native sessions, benchmarks, shell commands, or child processes were launched. Existing files were read through filesystem APIs; existing PNGs were inspected directly. Impeccable product-state/readability criteria informed the review; its setup scripts and document-generation workflow were not run under the explicit review-only constraint.

**Outcome: three bounded UI issues to address; no demonstrated regression in completed-cover material restoration or structure orientation.** Findings below concern current source unless explicitly identified as capture evidence. No new macrofeatures are proposed.

## Findings

1. **P2 — The permanent construction-halt warning is truncated before its consequence.** In [field-construction.png](field-construction.png), the selected engineer's status reads `Assigned: TACTICAL COVER · new orders …`. `hud.gd:253–258` puts the warning after the structure name, then `hud.gd:390` truncates it into a single 234-pixel line. Yet `field_ops.gd:118–125` permanently pauses the site on accepted replacement orders/Stop, and `proposal` refuses another project until cancellation. The guide only says the engineer must stay at the work point (`hud.gd:507`); it does not explain that returning cannot resume a halted site. The same narrow presentation truncates the trailing refund information in the selected-site string (`hud.gd:401–405`, only 156 pixels). This hides consequential order and spending information, not decorative copy. **Fix:** lead with a short complete warning such as `New orders halt this site`; put assignment on a separate line/tooltip. After interruption, show `Click site → Cancel to rebuild`. Give the refund its own short line or a complete Cancel tooltip rather than appending it after progress and work time. Preserve the existing simulation rule.

2. **P2 — Suppressed builders receive the wrong recovery instruction.** `field_ops.gd:163–167` stops progress when the engineer is already at the work point but suppression is above 0.6, or no eligible living member is sufficiently close and unsuppressed. `hud.gd:246–251` checks death, reassignment, and BUILDING, then always says `Engineer must reach work point`; the world label at `hud.gd:192` similarly says `BUILDER NEEDED`. For a builder already there under fire, those instructions imply a movement problem. Reissuing a move then permanently halts the project under finding 1. **Evidence:** direct source-path comparison, not a reproduced native interaction. **Fix:** derive a short non-mutating status from the existing gates: en route, suppressed, members regrouping, reassigned, or lost. Do not recommend a new order for temporary suppression.

3. **P2 — The current Riverside briefing is drawn as one unbounded line.** `hud.gd:486–490` uses a 700-pixel modal and draws the full scenario brief at 16 pixels with `text_at`; `text_at` passes width `-1` to `draw_string` (`hud.gd:46–47`). The current `resources/scenarios.json` brief contains the two-sector/60-second objective plus reserve, engineering, starting-supply and income instructions in a paragraph over 200 characters long. There is no wrap, width limit or second line, so the paragraph exceeds the modal and can lose its trailing instructions offscreen at the configured 1440×900 viewport. **Evidence:** source layout and current string; a guide capture was not supplied. **Fix:** use a short one-line objective and put the existing supply/build rules in the guide rows, or wrap the brief within the modal and adjust the selector/row positions. Recheck the guide after the isolated benchmark, not during it.

## Checks and bounded passes

- Inspected [engineer menu](field-engineer-menu.png), [construction](field-construction.png), and [completed cover](field-completed-cover.png). They show field-work prices/times, 900→840 supply on cover placement, a translucent unfinished site and builder route, then an opaque sandbag assembly with engineer controls available again. Still frames establish these appearances, not the input sequence or elapsed-work correctness.
- Current `battle.gd:370–372` fixes structure rotation to zero and calls `FieldModels.sync_construction`. `field_models.gd:66–78` caches completed materials before ghost substitution, restores them at zero work remaining, and hides its construction bar at completion (`:86`). The completed capture supports the material-restoration result. No live transition or all-four-model orientation test was run.
- Placement dispatch calls authoritative `FieldOps.build`, reports `build_reason` on failure, and leaves placement active for retry (`battle.gd:537–547`). Esc clears placement. Structure movement is filtered by `orderable`; mobile selection excludes structures. The selected-site Cancel action passes the site ID to `FieldOps.cancel`; completed sites disable that button. These are source checks, not exercised clicks.
- Supply shortage and deployment cooldown have explicit hover/toast reasons. Placement preview displays the authoritative site reason and footprint. Physical spawn blockage falls back to the generic `Unable to deploy reinforcement`; no evidence here establishes a serious spending/state defect in that path.
- The supplied captures retain the earlier objective-label overlap. Current source uses wider badges with right-aligned status (`hud.gd:213–217`); portrait loading has a file-image fallback (`:39–44`). These lead fixes were inspected in source, not re-rendered. Cached minimap background usage and live fog/unit overlays are present (`:434–478`); no performance claim is made.
